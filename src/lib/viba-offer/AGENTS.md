- Own VIBA offer calculation (house m², hourly, custom/VBF) runs only through the pure `calc-model.ts`; the quote panel and Timothy `crm_viba_offer_calculate` share it and the `calc-schema.ts` input, so amounts are never computed by the model or a second path.
- The engine never fills rates, surcharges, norms or package content: missing rate = missing price, every rate needs a source note, unverified references need per-line acknowledgement, cost basis needs an explicit markup or margin — unapproved assumptions must not become prices.
- Items are written only by explicit user action through the existing `useUpsert("quote_items")` path; the plan appends, skips identical items and reports same-name conflicts, never updates or deletes existing items, so reruns are safe and quote totals stay DB-trigger computed.
- Customer-facing item names and scope text must pass `internalLeaks`; cost, margin, contingency and sources stay in the panel and the internal CSV only.
- Calculations are not persisted server-side (browser draft per user+quote only); durable calculation versions, `pricing_mode` and an approved rate card need the consolidated, not yet migrated plan in docs/system-map/p7-migracio/ (server-written, immutable rows).
